Our Story

A Family Legacy

Lifelong California residents, Troy and Cheri Pryor, along with their children Cody, Nathan and Lindsay, moved to the Sacramento area in the fall of 1997. They have enjoyed calling Rosemont their home since 2001.

The Heart Behind CAP's

CAP's Pizza and Tap House is a tribute to Cody, who passed away on July 3, 2016. Every aspect of the restaurant was designed with Cody in mind, from the name (CAP - Cody Allen Pryor) to the rustic, casual décor and the fun environment. Cody was most happy when he was hanging out with friends and family.

We know that we have built a place he would have been proud and excited about. We are equally excited to be in the Rosemont Community and look forward to serving you!

"Family-owned, community-focused, serving memories one slice at a time."
